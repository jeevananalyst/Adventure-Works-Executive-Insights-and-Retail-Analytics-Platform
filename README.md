## 📌 Project Overview

**Adventure Works Executive Insights & Retail Analytics Platform** is an interactive Power BI business intelligence solution designed to transform retail and customer data into actionable business insights.

The report provides an executive-level view of business performance while enabling deeper analysis across **products, customers, geography, categories, and key business drivers**.

The dashboard was designed with a focus on **business storytelling, interactive analysis, KPI monitoring, data exploration, and decision support**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to provide a centralized analytical platform that enables stakeholders to:

* Monitor overall business performance
* Track key revenue and profitability indicators
* Analyze product and category performance
* Understand customer behavior and contribution
* Identify geographical performance patterns
* Investigate factors influencing business outcomes
* Drill down from executive KPIs into detailed operational insights
* Support data-driven business decisions

## 📊 Dashboard Architecture

The solution consists of multiple analytical pages, each designed for a specific business requirement.

### 1. 🏢 Executive Dashboard

The executive dashboard provides a high-level overview of business performance through:

* KPI cards
* Revenue and performance indicators
* Trend analysis
* Category comparisons
* Interactive filters
* Performance breakdowns

Designed for executives and business stakeholders who need a quick understanding of current business performance.

---

### 2. 🌎 Geographic / Map Analysis

The geographic analysis page provides location-based insights using interactive mapping.

Users can:

* Explore performance by geography
* Identify regional patterns
* Compare geographical contribution
* Interactively filter the analysis

This helps identify geographical opportunities and performance variations.

---

### 3. 📦 Product Details

The Product Details page enables detailed product-level analysis.

Key analytical components include:

* Product performance KPIs
* Product-level trends
* Gauge-based performance indicators
* Category/product filtering
* Historical performance analysis

This page helps identify high-performing and underperforming products.

---

### 4. 👥 Customer Detail

The Customer Detail page provides a deeper understanding of customer-level performance.

The analysis includes:

* Customer KPIs
* Customer segmentation
* Customer-level trends
* Interactive filtering
* Detailed customer tables
* Donut-chart based customer analysis

This allows stakeholders to investigate customer contribution and behavior.

---

### 5. 🌳 Decomposition Tree

The Decomposition Tree provides an interactive way to investigate business performance across multiple dimensions.

It allows users to break down a selected metric and explore contributing dimensions to identify **where performance is coming from and what factors influence it**.

This provides a more analytical approach than static charts and supports root-cause exploration.

---

### 6. 🔍 Key Influencers

The Key Influencers analysis uses Power BI's analytical capabilities to identify factors associated with changes in important business metrics.

This helps answer questions such as:

* What factors are associated with higher performance?
* Which customer/product attributes influence the selected metric?
* Which segments behave differently from the overall population?

This page adds an analytical and diagnostic layer to the dashboard.

---
